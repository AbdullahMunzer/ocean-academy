About IOI Ocean Academy Bangladesh

The International Ocean Institute (IOI) is a world-renowned non-governmental ocean governance body founded in 1972 by Prof. Elisabeth Mann Borgese. The IOI Ocean Academy Bangladesh serves as the national academic vehicle delivering ocean literacy, marine spatial knowledge, and maritime governance capacity to students, researchers, youth, and coastal decision-makers across the Bay of Bengal delta.

Curricular Focus

  • UNCLOS 1982 & Maritime delimitation of Bangladesh
  • Swatch of No Ground (SoNG) & Marine Protected Areas
  • Sundarbans mangrove ecology & blue carbon preservation
  • Sustainable blue economy & mariculture innovation
  • Marine pollution, microplastics & coastal resilience
